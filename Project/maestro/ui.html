Google Checking…

Reading the local Google status.

Sign-in happens on Google's own page in your browser. MAESTRO never asks for your Google password.
Set up Google first. Connect stays off until MAESTRO has your own OAuth client:
  1. In Google Cloud Console, create an OAuth client of type Desktop app.
  2. Save its JSON file as ~/.maestro/google/client_secret.json.
  3. Click Refresh status. Full guide: docs/GOOGLE-SETUP.md.

Disconnect Google?

  • MAESTRO's Google sign-in stored on this computer will be deleted.
  • MAESTRO will also ask Google to revoke its access (this needs the internet).
  • Your Gmail messages, Drive files and Calendar events are not deleted or changed.

About voice input

Voice libraries
…
  • Audio is processed on this computer and is never uploaded or saved.
  • The first recording may take longer while the Whisper speech model loads; the very first time it may be downloaded.
  • Microphone permission belongs to the app running MAESTRO (for example Terminal or VS Code).
  • Recording stops by itself after 60 seconds.
